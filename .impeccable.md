## Design Context

### Users
Five roles on one local app at the EAFIT parking lot (Las Vegas and Regional accesses): drivers in the queue (mostly on phones), the gate operator watching two lanes, valets, the key office, and a jury watching the demo. Jobs: know position and honest wait, admit the right vehicle, track valet service, hand keys only against a ticket, replay official scenarios.

### Brand Personality
Ordered, regal, calm. The brand speaks in the header and in the license plates; the rest of the screen works. Confidence through clarity, never urgency through color.

### Aesthetic Direction
Light operational canvas tinted toward the logo navy, with Liquid Glass reserved for floating layers (bar, mobile dock, toast, next-step, valet ticket, simulation controls, dialogs). Palette fixed by the official logo: navy #212D3D and gold #A98E4C plus derived tones; state colors are a semantic extension. Anti-references: SaaS card kit with identical shadows, all-caps eyebrows, side-stripe callouts, emoji iconography, glass on dense data.

### Design Principles
1. Identity is fixed: logo and palette are sources of truth, never reinterpreted.
2. Operational hierarchy first: the next action sits next to the object it acts on.
3. Animate only confirmed state changes; transform and opacity only.
4. State is always text + symbol + color.
5. Glass is a material for floating layers, not a decoration for data.
